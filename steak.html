Steak Recipe

Description:

Juicy steak with butter melting off it, perfect for breakfast, lunch, and dinner!

Ingredients:

  • 1 and 1/2 pounds of ribeye steak
  • 2 tablespoons of olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 tablespoons of butter
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • Fresh rosemary or thyme (optional)

Steps:

  1. Preheat your grill or skillet to high heat.
  2. Season the steak generously with salt and pepper on both sides.
  3. Drizzle olive oil over the steak and rub it in.
  4. Place the steak on the grill or skillet and cook for 4-5 minutes on each side for medium-rare, or adjust time for desired doneness.
  5. In the last minute of cooking, add butter, garlic, and herbs to the pan and baste the steak with the melted butter.
  6. Remove the steak from heat and let it rest for 5 minutes before slicing and serving.
