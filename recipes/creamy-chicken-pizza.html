Creamy Chicken Pizza

Description:

This creamy chicken pizza is a delicious and comforting dish that combines tender chicken, creamy sauce, and gooey cheese on a crispy pizza crust.

Ingredients:

  • 1 pizza crust (store-bought or homemade)
  • 1 cup cooked chicken, shredded
  • 1/2 cup cream cheese, softened
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/4 cup sour cream
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Fresh parsley or basil for garnish (optional)

Steps:

  1. Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. In a bowl, mix together the cream cheese, mayonnaise, sour cream, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and black pepper until smooth.
  3. Spread the creamy mixture evenly over the pizza crust.
  4. Top with shredded chicken and sprinkle with mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven for 12-15 minutes or until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  6. Remove from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes before slicing.
  7. Garnish with fresh parsley or basil if desired. Serve warm and enjoy!
